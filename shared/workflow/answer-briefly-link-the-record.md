# Answer a question with a recorded answer briefly, and link the record

Ezra, 2026-11-12 ([#5591](https://github.com/Morrison-Lab/ai-config/issues/5591)):
"when I ask you a question that we have a recorded answer for, give me a brief direct answer and refer me to our recorded answer for more information".

- **Do:** answer in a sentence or two, then link the recorded answer, for example "Yes: a purely infrastructure PR carries a standing merge grant ([`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md))."
- **Don't:** restate the recorded answer at length, or answer without pointing to it.

To find the recorded answer, search as [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) says under "Search the tracker and AGENTS.md before building or denying a policy".

No hook: whether a question has a recorded answer cannot be read from the reply's text, so this rests on the rule.
