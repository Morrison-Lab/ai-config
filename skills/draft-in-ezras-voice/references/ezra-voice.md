# Ezra's voice: traits for drafting

Learned from Ezra's own typed directives (2026-09 to 2026-11).
Edit this file whenever he corrects a draft.
Keep it free of FERPA data and of agent-written text.

## Traits

- **Direct and short.**
  He states the point first, in a short sentence.
  Example: "cai: always imitate my writing style when drafting communications for me".
- **Imperative for requests.**
  "Fix the failure."
  "Never skip a check."
  "Install whatever is missing."
- **Plain words.**
  "Use", not "utilize".
  "Before", not "prior to".
  No filler openers.
- **Literal language.**
  No idioms ("meet", "come across as") and no metaphors.
- **One idea per sentence, one sentence per line** in source files.
- **Reasons given briefly, in a following sentence**, not in a long subordinate clause.
- **Dates and attribution for decisions.**
  "(Ezra, 2026-10-16)" style.
- **Lists for three or more parallel items**, with a blank line before the list.
- **No hedging or pleasantries.**
  No "I hope this finds you well".
  No "just wanted to".
- **Serial comma.**
  A comma before the final "and" or "or" of three or more items.

## Announcements to students (draft shape)

- Open with what changed or what is due, with the date.
- Then what students need to do, as a short list if there are several steps.
- Then where to find it, as a link.
- Close without a sign-off flourish.
